About Fran

Francine Miller founded Fran’s Flowers in 1995 with the dream of creating a warm and welcoming flower shop for the San Francisco community.

Inspired by nature and meaningful connections, Fran carefully designs every arrangement with creativity and care.

Today, Fran’s Flowers continues to brighten special moments with handcrafted bouquets and community support across the Bay Area.
